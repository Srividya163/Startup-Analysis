# ✅Title: 
Competitor Analysis: Why One Startup Failed and One Succeeded – A Data Analyst’s Perspective
# ✅Goal:
To investigate two startups in the same domain and understand why one succeeded and the other failed by using data-driven evidence.
# 🚀 Objectives:

- Collect and clean data about both startups.

- Analyze business performance indicators (funding, market share, pricing, reviews, traffic, social media engagement, etc.).

- Compare the business strategies and identify key differentiators.

- Present insights explaining the success vs failure outcome.

# ✅Analysis:
Flipkart gained greater user traction due to its wider range of trending brands, intuitive interface, and frequent discount offers, unlike Snapdeal, which lacked these competitive advantages"— showing how market competition can reshape dynamics even in growth years.
